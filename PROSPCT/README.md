# Prospct

**Find. Organize. Reach.**

Prospct is a B2B lead database that helps you discover, manage, and reach the right business contacts. With access to **60+ million contacts**, you can build your ideal list in minutes — no spreadsheets, no guesswork.

---

## What you can do

### Find leads
Search 60+ million business contacts and filter by country, city, industry, job title, seniority, company size, keywords, and more. Looking for SaaS founders in Berlin, or marketing managers at mid-sized companies? A few clicks and they're on your screen.

### Organize your lists
Save contacts and companies into custom lists and folders. Keep track of who you've contacted, who's a new lead, and who's already saved. Revisit any saved search anytime.

### Enrich your data
Fill in the blanks. Prospct can complete missing company details like website, social links, and more — so your records are always ready to use.

### Verify emails
Make sure your messages actually land. Verify a single email instantly, or upload a whole list and watch the results live as they come in.

### Export & import
Export filtered leads to CSV or Excel with exactly the columns you need, or import your own data to keep everything in one place.

### Manage billing & credits
Work on a simple credit system — you only pay for what you use. Choose from flexible plans, apply coupons, and see every credit you spend in your history.

### Work as a team
Invite teammates to your workspace. Owners, admins, and members each get the right level of access, and everyone works from the same lists and credits.

---

## How it works

1. **Sign up** with your email, Google, LinkedIn, or Telegram.
2. **Search** the database and apply filters until you have the right list.
3. **Save** what's useful, enrich and verify your data.
4. **Export** your leads and get going.

New to Prospct? Sign up and explore the search — most filters are free to try. Unlock the full set with a paid plan.

---

## One platform for it all

Prospct comes with everything built in:

- **User dashboard** — search, lists, verification, billing, team settings.
- **Admin console** — manage users, plans, deals, subscriptions, and see revenue analytics.
- **Live updates** — get real-time notifications right in the app.
- **Secure payments** — trusted gateways for a safe checkout experience.
